- regression_asset: tests/ux-checks.browser.mjs check UXF-019

The hero section is vertically centred in a block about one screen tall. Above the kicker there is
empty space of 290 px (ess, 1024x768) and 200 px (connectors, 1366x768), so the heading, paragraph and
terminal start low and the "Get started" button lands on or under the bottom edge at the laptop sizes
the gates use (1366x768, 1280x720, 1024x768). The one next action is not on the first screen of any of
the six sites at 1024x768; at 1366x768 it is cut on connectors and not observed on the others in a
full-size shot. All six sites share the hero component. Their bots needed a PageDown on the landing page
before the click (ess 1280x800: `PageDown > click "Get started"`, report.md).

Sessions: every landing page at 1024x768 and 1280x720, six sites. Cost: S.

Fix: top-align the hero (no `min-height` centring) so heading and button sit within the first 700 px.
