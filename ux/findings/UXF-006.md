# UXF-006: the docs sidebar leaves the viewport with its column as the footer comes into view

- id: UXF-006
- title: The docs sidebar leaves the viewport with its column as the footer comes into view
- class: layout
- severity: S3
- metric: layout.hidden
- status: open, outside R-01's change set; the adapter checks the sidebar only while the footer is out of view
- found_by: R-01 round command (always-visible check), confirmed by the R-01 report reviewer
- evidence_recording: none (no recordings in R-01)
- evidence_screenshot: ux/rounds/R-01/shots/pages/page-docs-adopt-1440x900-light.png and the reviewer's re-measurement (review-report.md § 2 item 1)
- evidence_lines: ux/rounds/R-01/report.md, "Failing checks", 1440x900 /docs/adopt one screen down; ux/rounds/R-01/review-report.md § 2 item 1
- regression_asset: none yet; the always-visible check holds the sidebar at every half-screen step while the footer is out of view

The sticky sidebar can only stay inside its column. During the last screen of every docs page the
column ends, the footer comes in, and the sidebar slides up under the navbar and out of view: the
report reviewer counted 16 views at the gating 1024x768 (8 docs pages, both themes) and 38 over the
desktop sizes. This is the standard Docusaurus docs layout; no CSS-only change keeps a sticky element
on screen past the end of its container.

The adapter marks the sidebar `whileFooterHidden`: gate 2 holds it at every half-screen step while
the footer is out of view, and lets it leave with its column once the footer shows. Changing that is
a layout decision (a sidebar that overlays the footer, or a fixed sidebar) for a later round.
