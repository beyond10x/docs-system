# UXF-022: at 1024 px the "On this page" column is squeezed to about 100 px and wraps each heading over three lines

- id: UXF-022
- title: At 1024 px the "On this page" column is squeezed to about 100 px and wraps each heading over three lines
- class: layout
- severity: S3
- metric: new, toc.width_1024 (width of the right-hand table of contents at 1024x768)
- status: open
- found_by: R-02 heuristic reviewer, heuristics 10 and 15
- evidence_recording: none (no recordings in R-02)
- evidence_screenshot: ux/rounds/R-02/shots/sites/ess/contact-sheet-light.png (1024x768 docs frame), ux/rounds/R-02/shots/sites/ess/contact-sheet-dark.png
- evidence_lines: ess /docs/reference/spec-versions at 1024x768: the right column is about 110 px wide, entries such as "ess-mutation-report/ and ess-mutation-manifest/ - the mutation audit" take five lines and code is broken after a hyphen; text about 11 px. Not observed at full size (no page shot at 1024 for docs); the same column at 1366x768 is wide enough (page-docs-1366x768-dark.png).
- regression_asset: none

Between the 996 px breakpoint and about 1200 px the theme keeps the sidebar (about 200 px), the content
and the table of contents side by side; the content wins, and the table of contents shrinks until its
entries stop reading as a list. Every site that has long or code-named headings shows it; ess does
most. The 1024x768 viewport is measured-only, so no gate sees it.

Sessions: ess, 1024x768, both themes; other sites not observed at full size. Cost: S.

Fix: hide the table of contents below 1200 px (the "On this page" bar of the phone layout already exists) or give it a floor of 180 px.
